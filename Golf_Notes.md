# Golf Notes

## 8/6/26 — Range with Jeromy Stansberry

Key takeaways:

1. Do not drop head in backswing, keep head still. This was causing tops.
2. Scoot a bit further back from the ball — will have to tweak this to get it right.
3. Line up to target, then drop right foot back ~3". This helps draw the ball.
4. Set up grip with neutral or slightly closed club face. With dropping foot back, too closed a face hooks the ball.
